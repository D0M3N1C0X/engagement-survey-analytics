What does your survey say, and what moves it?

Load the answers, one row per person. You get each dimension's favourable score with an honest interval, the eNPS, what drives engagement, and results by team, with every group under five respondents hidden.

Your file stays on this computer: it is read by this page and sent nowhere. Go offline after the page has loaded and it still works.

1Your survey answers

An Excel or CSV export with one row per person: a team or department column and the questions scored 1 to 5. A questionnaire file (item, dimension, type) is optional and saves you naming the dimensions.

2Check the columns

Questions scored 1 to 5, and the dimension each belongs to. Clear a name to leave a question out.

Dimension scores

The share of each person's answers that are favourable (4 or 5), averaged over people. The interval resamples whole teams, because people in a team answer alike.

What drives engagement

Relative weights (Johnson's method): each dimension's share of the variation in engagement the dimensions explain together. Dimensions move together, so this is the honest way to rank them; it shows where to look, not what causes what.

By team

Favourable scores per team. Teams under five respondents are never shown, not even their number of respondents.
